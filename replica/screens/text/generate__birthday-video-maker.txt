Birthday video questions
How do I make a first birthday video?
Add your favorite photos and short clips from the first year, put them in the order you want, and add a name and birthday message. You do not need a photo from every month. The same editor works for children, parents, and friends of any age.
Can I use my own music and birthday wishes?
Choose a built-in soundtrack or add an audio file you have permission to use. The editor preview is silent; music is included in the exported video. Play the finished video to check the sound before sharing.
Will this change the faces in my photos?
The photo slideshow uses your original photos with layout, text, timing and transitions. Create an image is a separate AI tool with an editable prompt. From an image preview, choose Generate video to open Baby Photo to Video, review the photo, model, prompt and credit price, then generate a clip.
What kind of video can I export?
Use up to 20 photos and clips for a video up to 90 seconds long. Export in portrait 9:16 or landscape 16:9 at 720p. Browsers that support MP4 recording export MP4; other supported browsers export WebM. The editor shows the format available in your browser.
