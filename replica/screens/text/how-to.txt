01 A photo to bring to life
Use the baby guide for a portrait or the video studio guide for a product, illustration or scene. Begin with one small movement; you can also create a new scene from text.

02 A voice recording or song
Use Talking Baby for a line of speech, Baby Podcast for a presenter or turn-by-turn conversation, and Baby Singing for an existing vocal recording. These workflows animate to audio you prepare.
Have ready : One visible face and a clean speech or singing track.

03 A dance or reaction to follow
A reference clip supplies the performance. Choose dance for body movement or a meme for a readable reaction, then add the final caption in your editor.
Have ready : A short motion clip and a photo with a compatible pose.

04 An image idea or a new style
Create a scene or edit a photo in the image studio. Use Cartoon Baby for a portrait style, or build a fruit character before adding motion in the fruit tutorial.
Have ready : A brief description and, for an edit, the original image.

05 An imagined family portrait or growth sequence
Parent photos create an imagined child portrait. A photo of one child starts age progression; the growth guide then shows how to arrange the stills into a video. These are two different tasks.
Have ready : Parent portraits, or one child portrait and the ages to explore.

06 A shortlist of baby names
Compare names with the full surname, the family’s languages and a few preferences. Learn how to check meanings and keep a shortlist you can discuss together.
Have ready : The full surname, naming culture and one or two priorities.
