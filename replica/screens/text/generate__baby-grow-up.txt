AI baby age progression depends on small identity cues: eye shape, cheeks, smile, face outline, hairline, and lighting. A charming snapshot is not always the strongest reference.
Use a portrait with readable facial structure
Use one clear face with eyes, nose, mouth, cheeks, and face outline visible.
Prefer soft, even lighting over strong backlight, heavy shadows, or dramatic filters.
Avoid pacifiers, bottles, hands, blankets, hats, and toys that cover the mouth or face shape.
Use the original photo when possible instead of a compressed screenshot or cropped social image.
Skip group photos and busy backgrounds when the goal is a recognizable grow-up preview.
For older ages, a calm expression often keeps identity more stable than an extreme smile.

Can AI show what my baby will look like when older?
It can create an imagined older version from a baby photo. Choose a target age to explore a possible appearance. Real growth depends on genetics and many other factors, so the result is for fun.
Can I see my baby as an adult at 18?
Yes. Select 18 as the target age to request a baby-to-adult preview. AI results vary, so compare both the apparent age and the resemblance to your original photo.
How do I see what my baby might look like in five years?
Set the target age to your child's current age plus five. For example, choose age 6 for a one-year-old. Selecting age 5 asks for a five-year-old result, rather than automatically adding five years.
Do I need photos of the parents?
No. This tool starts with one photo of an existing baby or child. To imagine a future baby from parent photos instead, use our AI baby generator .
Do I need to download an age progression app?
No. You can choose a photo, set an age and generate the image on this website from your phone or computer.
