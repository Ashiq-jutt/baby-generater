How to Make a Baby Podcast Video
The Baby Podcast tool animates a baby portrait to match your audio. Add a photo, upload or record a short voice clip, then choose the resolution and review the credits before generating.
Build a short episode around one clear idea. Prepare the voice first, choose a portrait with a visible mouth, then animate that portrait to the recording. A microphone in the picture sets the scene; the uploaded audio supplies the actual voice.

Turn an idea into a short recording
Use a setup, one thought and a closing line. Read the script aloud and time the recording instead of guessing duration from the number of words.
A tiny host monologue
Welcome to the tiny podcast. Today’s question: why do socks disappear? I put two on this morning. Now I have one sock and a very suspicious teddy bear. Record this as one voice. Pause after the question and before the final line. The example is a script to record, not text you can paste into this audio-only editor.
A family milestone recap
This week I learned to crawl across the room. Everyone cheered. I was just trying to reach my favorite toy. Keep the pace conversational. Record a clean ending rather than cutting immediately after the final word. You can use the same structure for a different milestone.
Prepare a host photo and clean voice track
Keep the mouth visible Choose one face, even lighting and a near-front view. Place a pretend studio microphone beside or below the face, not across the lips. Leave room around the head for small movements and around the frame for later captions.
Record or upload the voice Record in a quiet room, with one speaker and a steady distance from the microphone. Listen for clipped words, room echo, music and long silences before generating. Prepare an external voice recording first if you need a voice other than your own; this editor has no text-to-speech or voice selector.
Check the input limits The current podcast workflow uses Kling Avatar, with audio up to 60 seconds and 20 MB. The file picker accepts MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC and OGG among audio files. If the browser cannot detect the duration, re-export a standard audio file before retrying.
Choose resolution and framing Choose 720p or 1080p and review the estimate after the audio duration is detected. Prepare the photo in the composition you want: this editor has no separate aspect-ratio selector. Trim the audio before upload if the test should be shorter.

Make a two-speaker episode from separate clips
The current tool animates one photo with one audio file per generation. It does not assign different voices to two faces in a group photo.
Split the conversation into turns Prepare a separate portrait for each host and a separate audio segment for each speaking turn. Keep the frame shape and visual style similar so cuts feel consistent.
Generate and inspect each turn Use only that speaker’s audio with that speaker’s portrait. Review the credit estimate for each clip. Download a usable turn before making the next one.
Assemble the episode in a video editor Place the clips in dialogue order and keep the pauses natural. Add captions and background music in the editor. Check that the original clip audio is not playing twice underneath a separate full-length voice track.
Review the voice, timing and final file
Mouth movement looks late Check at the first sentence and near the end. If the source has overlapping speech or loud music, prepare a cleaner single-speaker recording. If the mouth is obstructed, change the portrait. Compare one change at a time.
The clip feels slow Shorten the introduction or remove a long pause from the audio before generating again. Changing a written script after recording does not change the audio already uploaded.
The beginning or ending is cut off Listen to the source file by itself. Restore a clipped word in the recording before retrying. Leave a small natural pause after the closing line, then trim the finished clip in your editor if necessary.
Check the export on a phone Open the downloaded file, listen at a normal volume and watch the whole clip. Add and proofread captions in an editor; verify names and punctuation manually. Keep captions away from the mouth and platform controls.

FAQ
Can I type a script in the Baby Podcast tool? ▼ This editor requires audio and has no text-to-speech input. Record your message here or prepare an audio file elsewhere and upload it.
What photo works best? ▼ Use one clear face, good lighting, minimal occlusion (no hands covering face), and a simple background.
How does audio length affect the price? ▼ Credits depend on the audio duration and selected resolution. Review the live estimate before generating; a shorter recording uses fewer credits at the same resolution.
Does the tool turn my voice into a baby voice? ▼ The recording supplies the voice. A baby portrait changes the visual character; it does not by itself change the speaker’s age or voice. Prepare the voice you want before uploading.
Can I add automatic subtitles here? ▼ The current podcast editor does not have a subtitle control. Download the generated video and add captions in a video editor, then check the transcription.
Can I make an episode longer than 60 seconds? ▼ Prepare shorter audio segments that each fit the current input limit, generate separate clips and join them in a video editor. Each generation has its own credit estimate.
