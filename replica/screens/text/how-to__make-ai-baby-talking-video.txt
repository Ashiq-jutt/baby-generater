How to Make a Baby Photo Talk
AI Baby Talking pairs a baby photo with uploaded or recorded audio. The recording provides the voice and timing. Choose a model and resolution, check the credits, then generate and download the talking video.
Make one photo deliver one clear message. The portrait supplies the face; your uploaded or recorded audio supplies the words, language and voice. Prepare both before choosing a model.

Write a line, then record it
The current editor accepts audio, not a typed script. Read these examples aloud, listen back and keep the version with clear words and a natural ending.
Record in a quiet room at a steady distance from the microphone. In the tool, choose the recording button or upload an existing file. Listen from the first word through the ending, then trim long silence in an audio editor before uploading again.
A personal greeting
Hi Grandpa! I saved my biggest smile for you. See you soon. Say the name clearly and pause between the greeting and the second sentence. Replace the recipient before recording, not after uploading the file.
A playful reply
I was not avoiding bedtime. I was waiting for the next story. Pause slightly before the second sentence. Use one voice without background music for the first attempt; add music when editing the finished video.
Match the recording to the model
Pruna AI Avatar The current default offers 720p and accepts audio up to 180 seconds. Start with a short line to inspect the face and timing before committing to a longer recording.
Kling Avatar Offers 720p or 1080p, with audio up to 60 seconds. Switching from Pruna does not shorten a longer recording: trim the source file and check the duration again.
Audio file and estimate The upload limit is 20 MB. Supported audio includes MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC and OGG. Wait for the duration to be detected, then review the credit estimate after choosing the model and resolution. If duration detection fails, re-export the audio in a standard format.
Photo composition Use one clear face with the lips visible and space around the head. Crop out distracting faces and avoid pacifiers or fingers across the mouth. Prepare the desired frame shape in the photo; this editor has no separate aspect-ratio control.

Check speech at three points
First word Play with sound and watch when the mouth starts moving. If the audio itself begins late, trim its opening silence. If a word is already clipped in the source, record that line again.
Middle of the line Look for mouth closures around sounds such as “m”, “b” and “p”, where they occur in your recording. Check that cheeks, eyes and glasses stay stable. This is a practical viewing check, not a guarantee of phoneme-perfect synchronization.
Last word and ending Watch through the final word rather than judging the thumbnail. If the face drifts during head turns, try a straighter portrait and a shorter message. Change one input at a time so the comparison remains useful.
Download a message that is ready to share
Save a usable result before trying another variation. Open the downloaded file and check both the picture and sound. For captions, trim or music, use a video editor; keep captions below the mouth and proofread names. Adding a separate copy of the voice track can create an echo if the original clip audio remains enabled.

Tips
Use a sharp, front-facing photo with clear facial features for best lip-sync results.
Record in a quiet place and use a steady speaking pace.
Start with a short recording to compare results before spending more credits.
Shorter clips (5-15 seconds) produce more natural-looking results.
Check the first words and the end of the recording when you preview the result. If speech is cut off, trim the source audio yourself and generate again after checking the new credit estimate.
FAQ
How do model and resolution affect AI baby talking cost? ▼ Each model and resolution has its own per-second rate. Use the live credit estimate in the editor to compare options before generating.
Can I type text to generate speech here? ▼ This editor requires audio and has no text-to-speech input. Record your message here or prepare an audio file elsewhere and upload it.
Why does the lip-sync look off? ▼ Common causes: low-quality photo, obstructed face, or fast speech. Use a clearer photo, reduce occlusion, and slow down the audio.
What photo works best for AI baby talking? ▼ Use a clear, well-lit, front-facing baby photo. Avoid hands, pacifiers, or anything covering the face. One face per photo works best.
Can I create a talking baby video for free? ▼ Check the credits available in your account and the current generation estimate. Available credits may cover a test, but this guide does not promise unlimited free generation. Longer recordings and different settings can change the cost.
Will changing the site language translate my recording? ▼ No. The audio supplies the spoken language. Prepare a recording in the language you want before uploading; changing the interface language does not translate or dub it.
Will the photo turn an adult voice into a baby voice? ▼ No. The photo supplies the visual character. Prepare the desired voice in the recording first; the current editor has no baby-voice conversion or text-to-speech selector.
