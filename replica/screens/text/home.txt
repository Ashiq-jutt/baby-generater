Tips for Better Future Baby Results
Use clear, front-facing photos.
A sharp selfie with both eyes, nose, mouth, and face shape visible gives the AI stronger parent-photo details.
Avoid blurry photos.
Motion blur and soft focus can hide eye shape, nose shape, lip detail, and facial balance from the baby face generator.
Remove sunglasses and face coverings.
Hats, sunglasses, masks, and heavy shadows can block the visible traits needed for a realistic future baby preview.
Avoid side profiles.
Front-facing parent photos usually work better than side views because the AI can see both eyes, cheeks, jawline, and face symmetry.

AI Baby Generator FAQ
What is an AI baby generator?
An AI baby generator creates a creative future baby preview from parent photos. It is designed for fun, curiosity, family sharing, and social posts.
How does an AI baby generator work?
The generator uses uploaded parent photos and visible facial traits to create a realistic baby-style portrait. The result is a baby face prediction for entertainment, not a scientific result.
Is this the same as a future baby generator or baby face predictor?
Yes. AI baby generator, future baby generator, baby face generator, and baby face predictor usually describe the same kind of creative parent-photo preview tool. On BabyVideo.ai, they all point to a fun future baby image made from visible traits in parent photos.
Can I use two parent photos?
Yes. You can upload one or two parent photos. Two clear parent photos usually give the AI more visible facial information to blend into a future baby preview.
Can I generate a baby boy or baby girl?
Yes. Choose baby boy, baby girl, or Surprise Me before generating. You can create different future baby versions from the same parent photos.

Is this a real genetic prediction?
No. AI baby generator results are creative previews for fun. They are not medical, genetic, or scientific predictions.
Are uploaded parent photos private?
Parent photos are used to create your AI baby preview and are not shown as public homepage examples. You should only upload photos you own or have permission to use.
What kind of photos work best?
Clear, front-facing parent photos with visible eyes, nose, mouth, forehead, cheeks, and jawline usually work best. Avoid sunglasses, heavy filters, side profiles, and group photos.
Can I use only one parent photo?
Yes. A single parent photo can create a creative baby preview, though two parent photos usually provide more visual context.
Why do future baby results look different each time?
Each generation creates one creative variation. Some visible traits may lean more toward one parent photo, while other traits may be softly blended, so running the AI baby generator again can produce a different preview.
