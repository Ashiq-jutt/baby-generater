3. Data Retention and Deletion
3.1 Uploaded Content - Not Stored on Our Servers
Your uploaded photos and videos are NOT stored on our servers. Here's how it works:
Files you upload are processed locally in your browser
They are converted to temporary data and directly sent to our AI processing partners
We do not store or retain your original uploaded files
Once processing is complete, the temporary data is discarded
3.2 Generated Content - 7 Day Retention
AI-generated content (videos and images) is automatically deleted after 7 days. This includes:
All AI-generated videos and images
Result URLs from our AI processing partners
We recommend downloading your generated content promptly, as it will not be recoverable after the 7-day retention period.

We retain your account information (email, usage history, credit balance) for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal, regulatory, or legitimate business purposes.

Comply with data protection best practices
