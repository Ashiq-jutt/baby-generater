Subscription Cancellation Policy
Last updated: 11.27, 2025
1. Cancellation Notice Period
Subscribers wishing to cancel their subscription are required to do so at least 24 hours before the start of the next billing cycle to avoid being charged for the upcoming period.
2. Refund Policy
If you encounter any issues with our product, please feel free to contact us at email@babyvideo.ai to discuss a potential refund. Refund requests will be evaluated individually based on the specifics of each case.
3. Prorated Charges
Cancellations partway through a billing cycle will not result in prorated charges. Subscribers will have access to the service until the end of the current billing cycle.
4. Auto-Renewal
Your subscription will automatically renew at the end of each billing cycle. You may opt out of auto-renewal by adjusting your subscription settings or by contacting us before the renewal date.
5. Immediate Cancellation Processing
All cancellations will be processed immediately upon request. However, the subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle.
6. Changes to the Policy
We reserve the right to modify or replace this policy at any time. If a revision is significant, we will provide at least 7 days' notice before any new terms take effect.
7. Contact Information
To initiate a cancellation or if you have any questions regarding this policy, please contact us at email@babyvideo.ai
