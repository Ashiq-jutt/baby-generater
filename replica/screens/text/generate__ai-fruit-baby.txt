Choose the fruit by the character you want people to feel
Most users do not need a long description. They need the right fruit name. Use the fruit as the character identity, then let the clip stay simple and readable.
Strawberry baby
Sweet, bright, instantly recognizable
Best for
birthday replies, family chat surprises, Valentine's posts, spring clips, and soft social moments.
Watch out
Use a clean photo because red texture and seed detail can compete with the face.
Banana baby
Funny, warm, and easy to read
Best for
playful short videos, silly reactions, kid-friendly humor, and clips that need a quick smile.
Watch out
A full-body or upper-body photo usually works better than a tight face crop.
Watermelon baby
Bold, summery, and colorful
Best for
summer posts, picnic themes, first-step style clips, and videos where the color needs to pop.
Watch out
Keep the background simple so green stripes and red fruit color do not make the frame busy.
Apple, peach, or orange baby
Soft, round, and portrait-friendly
Best for
family-safe clips, gentle greetings, cozy profile-style videos, and first tests with a new baby photo.
Watch out
These fruits are cleaner, so the expression and lighting matter more than the costume detail.
Durian or pineapple baby
Textured, funny, and more meme-ready
Best for
scroll-stopping character posts, comparison clips, and users who want a bolder fruit baby look.
Watch out
Spikes and texture can overwhelm the face. Use a centered, well-lit photo.
Grape or mango baby
Cute, soft, and easy to theme
Best for
warm family-share clips, simple tropical ideas, and fruit baby series where each character has a different mood.
Watch out
If the fruit shape is less obvious, pair it with a cleaner outfit, plain background, and close framing.

Use the photo as the character base
The baby photo gives the face, pose, and body context. Pick a source image that still looks clear after a fruit costume is added.
Face-forward photos help the smile, eyes, and cheeks stay readable.
Upper-body or full-body photos work better when you want tiny hands, sitting poses, or more body movement.
Type one fruit, not a sentence
The fruit name should carry the whole visual identity. A simple fruit choice is usually stronger than a vague theme.
Use clear fruit names such as strawberry, banana, apple, watermelon, mango, pineapple, grape, or durian.
Test one fruit at a time so you can compare which character reads fastest.

Use 9:16 for Reels, Shorts, and TikTok style clips.
Use 1:1 when the clip is mainly for family chat, profile previews, or a compact social post.

Pick a baby photo that can survive the fruit costume
AI fruit baby is more stylized than a normal baby video. The source photo has to leave room for fruit color, shape, and character detail without hiding the baby-like expression.
The photo has to leave room for fruit shape
Good source photo
One baby is the clear main subject.
Eyes, mouth, cheeks, and face outline are visible.
Hands or upper body are visible if you want a more animated character.
The background is calm enough for bright fruit color.
Harder source photo
Pacifiers, blankets, or hands cover the mouth and cheeks.
Several people appear in the frame.
Heavy filters, dark shadows, or strong backlight flatten the face.
Busy toys, patterned blankets, or clutter fight with the fruit costume.

Improve a weak fruit baby result
Do not judge the first clip only by sharpness. First check whether the fruit baby idea is clear at a glance. If the face, fruit shape, body, or background feels wrong, adjust the input before generating again.
The baby face gets lost
Choose a brighter photo where the face is larger, or use a smoother fruit shape such as apple, peach, orange, or banana.
The fruit is not obvious
Pick a fruit with stronger color or silhouette, such as strawberry, banana, watermelon, pineapple, or durian.
The body looks awkward
Use an upper-body or full-body photo so the AI has shoulders, hands, and sitting posture to work from.
The frame feels too noisy
Crop closer, use a cleaner background, and avoid toys, patterned blankets, or wide scenes with too much detail.
